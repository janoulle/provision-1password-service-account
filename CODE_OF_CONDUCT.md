# Code of conduct

Participate with respect, patience, and technical honesty. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberately unsafe guidance are not
acceptable.

Keep security reports private until a fix and disclosure plan are ready.
Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may restrict participation
when necessary to protect contributors and users.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
Good-faith reports will be reviewed without retaliation.
